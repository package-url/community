## Notes

- Meeting minutes are being kept and will be published, and the meeting will be recorded with Google Meet video and Gemini "note-taking".
- Our code of conduct (link in agenda above) applies to this meeting.
- Introductions.
- Summary
  - The meeting was focused on the new ABNF grammar for PURL and updates for the pending submission of VERS 1st Edition and PURL 2nd Edition (ECMA-427) standards to Ecma.
  - The group decided to eliminate the single equality sign in VERS version constraints to simplify parsing.
  - The group discussed the status of updates to the huggingface PURL type.
  - A leaner Augmented Backus-Naur Form grammar was introduced by Jan Kowalleck. This will be published as a new informative Annex for ECMA-427 2nd Edition (PURL).
  - The group confirmed that PURL conformance requires conformance with currently registered PURL type rules.
  - Discussions highlighted the prioritization of software identity over locatability for PURL dependency management and vulnerability use cases and the trade-offs between requiring qualifiers for locatability for some PURL types versus the dependency and vulnerability use cases.
- **Meeting Governance and Minutes**: Michael Herzog opened the meeting by confirming it is governed by the ECMA TC54 code of conduct and noted that while meetings are recorded to verify minutes, the recordings are not published. The community minutes from the 2026-08-05 meeting were presented, no objections were raised, and the minutes were deemed approved.
- **Equality Comparator Issue**: Immanuel Kunz raised an issue regarding a discrepancy in the specification concerning the use of an equality sign in version strings, specifically whether "vers:npm/1.2.3." and "vers:npm/=1.2.3." are considered equal. Michael Herzog and Immanuel Kunz reached a consensus to eliminate the option for a single equal sign in version constraints to simplify parsing, and Immanuel Kunz agreed to submit a pull request for this improvement.
- **Normalization and Canonical Form**: Jaime Rodríguez-Guerra discussed concerns regarding normalization, noting that other systems like 'conda' handle trailing zeros differently using fuzzy equality. Michael Herzog clarified that the current goal is to define a "canonical form" for the specification, rather than addressing fuzzy equality, which remains outside the scope of the immediate effort.
- **Huggingface PURL Type Status**: Jon Moroney inquired about the status of the huggingface PURL type, which has been dormant and is currently using an outdated .rst format. Michael Herzog explained that this issue is currently blocked by a lack of resources to perform updates, and Jon decided to take the matter back to their team to address the update internally.
- **Project Tracking Updates**: Michael Herzog provided an update on current tracking projects, noting that the ECMA-427 2nd Edition project is down to 18 items with 3 completed, while the VERS 1st Edition project is waiting for final input from Philippe Ombredanne regarding the VERS type schema.
- **VERS Type Schema and Documentation**: Jaime Rodríguez-Guerra expressed interest in contributing a conda type for VERS, but Michael Herzog advised waiting until the schema is finalized. Michael Herzog emphasized that they are seeking to keep the schema simple and prefer utilizing separate documentation files for narrative text rather than embedding such information within the JSON schema.
- **Introduction of ABNF Grammar**: Jan Kowalleck introduced a new, leaner Augmented Backus-Naur Form (ABNF) grammar for PURL, which was developed from scratch based on lessons learned over the past year. The grammar covers canonical PURL structures, including UTF-8 references and specific character sets required for accurate parsing.  (See https://github.com/package-url/purl-spec/pull/994 and its predecessor https://github.com/package-url/purl-spec/pull/578.)
- **Grammar Documentation and Structure**: Michael Herzog confirmed that the new grammar will be included as an informative annex in the submission for ECMA-427 2nd Edition. A separate document, to be named "lenient grammar," will be published on the website as informative guidance, similar to the existing "how-to-parse" documentation.
- **Grammar Feedback and Review**: Michael Herzog suggested reaching out to past contributors, specifically Daniel Scanteianu, to gather feedback on the new ABNF grammar. Jan Kowalleck noted that the grammar is condensed and ready for neutral review. John Horan commented on the use of "further restricts" versus "prohibits" in the grammar text; Michael Herzog invited John Horan to document this feedback in the relevant issue for quick resolution.
- **Conformance and Type Requirements**: Jon Moroney asserted that PURL is "insufficient" without types, arguing that the system's value lies in acting as an interchange for software identity. Michael Herzog acknowledged this perspective and discussed the ongoing challenge of defining "conformance" when balancing syntax checks against full type-level compliance, particularly for new tools.
- **Locatability versus Identity**: The group discussed whether a PURL must be a locator. Michael Herzog and Jan Kowalleck noted that while a PURL is defined as a locator, many critical use cases—such as vulnerability and dependency management—prioritize software identity over the ability to resolve the location.
- **Mandatory Repository Qualifiers**: A discussion took place regarding potential requirements for repository qualifiers. Jan Kowalleck suggested that defining required qualifiers per type within the schema could address some of the issues regarding location. Michael Herzog emphasized the need to base these requirements on specific, documented use cases rather than making them an absolute requirement that would alienate users who cannot provide location data.
- **Industry Participation**: Michael Herzog highlighted the need for more industry input to complement the current open-source-heavy participation. Jon Moroney mentioned their transition to a startup called Socket. Michael Herzog noted that they would look to engage with commercial tools that generate custom PURLs to help refine the specification and usage.
- **Meeting Conclusion and Next Steps**: Michael Herzog stated that the plan is to finalize the ABNF grammar and prepare the documentation for the TG2 meeting scheduled for this coming Friday. The meeting concluded with gratitude toward Jan Kowalleck for the significant work on the grammar, and Michael Herzog noted they would be coordinating with Immanuel Kunz for further scrutiny of VERS-related issues.
- The meeting was adjourned.
